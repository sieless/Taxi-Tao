## Taxi Services

### How do I book a taxi?
You can book a taxi through our website booking form, by calling +254 710 450 640, or through our mobile app available on the Play Store.

### What areas do you serve?
TaxiTao serves all 47 counties in Kenya. We have active services in major towns including Nairobi, Mombasa, Kisumu, Nakuru, Eldoret, Machakos, and many more.

### Are your drivers professional?
Yes, all TaxiTao drivers are vetted, licensed, and trained to provide professional service. Our drivers undergo background checks and regular vehicle inspections.

### Do you operate 24/7?
Yes, our taxi services are available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, including public holidays.

### What payment methods do you accept?
We accept M-Pesa and cash payments. Car hire bookings may require a deposit via M-Pesa.

## Car Hire

### What documents do I need for car hire?
You need a valid driving license, national ID or passport, and a refundable security deposit. Terms may vary by vehicle type.

### Can I hire a car without a driver?
Yes, we offer self-drive options for qualified drivers. Chauffeur-driven options are also available.

### What is the minimum hire period?
Our minimum hire period is 24 hours. We offer daily, weekly, and monthly rental packages.

### Do you offer airport transfers?
Yes, we provide reliable airport transfer services to JKIA, Wilson Airport, Mombasa Airport, Kisumu Airport, Eldoret Airport, and Malindi Airport.

### What types of vehicles do you have?
We offer sedans (Toyota Fielder, Mercedes C200), vans (Toyota HiAce), SUVs, and executive vehicles. Specific availability varies by location.
