Ngong Town sits at the foot of the Ngong Hills in Kajiado County. It serves as a key transport link between Nairobi and Kajiado's growing suburban areas.

## Transport in Ngong

Ngong has experienced rapid residential growth as part of the greater Nairobi expansion, creating high demand for commuter and local transport.
