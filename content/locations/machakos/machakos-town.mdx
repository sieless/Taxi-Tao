Machakos Town is the capital of Machakos County, located about 64 km southeast of Nairobi. It is a major transport hub linking Nairobi to the Ukambani region and the lower eastern Kenya.

## Transport in Machakos Town

Machakos Town serves as a key transit point for travelers heading to Kitui, Makueni, and the coastal region. The town has grown rapidly, creating high demand for reliable taxi and car hire services.

### Taxi Services
- 24/7 availability throughout Machakos Town and environs
- Airport transfers to JKIA and Wilson Airport
- Inter-town routes: Machakos to Nairobi, Machakos to Kitui, Machakos to Wote

### Car Hire
- Self-drive options for exploring the Ukambani region
- Chauffeur-driven executive cars for business travel
- Long-term rentals for corporate clients
