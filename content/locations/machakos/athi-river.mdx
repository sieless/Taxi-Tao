Athi River is a growing industrial and residential town along the Nairobi-Mombasa highway in Machakos County. It is part of the greater Nairobi metropolitan area.

## Transport in Athi River

Athi River has experienced rapid urbanization with the expansion of industrial zones and residential estates. The town's strategic location along the Mombasa Road makes it a key transport corridor.

### Taxi Services
- Quick pickups within Athi River, Mlolongo, Syokimau, and Kitengela
- Reliable connections to Nairobi CBD and JKIA
- Affordable rates for daily commuting
