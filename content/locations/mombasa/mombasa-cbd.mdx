Mombasa CBD is the coastal commercial hub of Kenya. As the country's second-largest city and premier tourist destination, Mombasa has unique transport needs.

## Transport in Mombasa

Mombasa serves millions of tourists annually in addition to its large resident population. Reliable transport is critical for both tourism and daily business operations.

- Hotel and resort transport
- Tour guide transport services
- City-wide daily commuting
