Malindi is a popular coastal tourist destination in Kilifi County, known for its beautiful beaches, Italian community, and historical sites like the Vasco da Gama Pillar.

## Transport in Malindi

Malindi's tourism-driven economy requires reliable transport for visitors exploring the coastal region.

### Taxi Services
- Airport transfers to Malindi Airport
- Hotel and resort transport throughout the coast
- Beach hopping and tourist attraction visits
- Inter-town routes to Watamu, Kilifi, and Mombasa

### Car Hire
- Self-drive vehicles for coastal exploration
- Safari cars for Tsavo East trips
- Executive vehicles for VIP tourists
- Weekly rental packages for holidaymakers
