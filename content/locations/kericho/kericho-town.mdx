Kericho Town is the tea-growing capital of Kenya, surrounded by vast tea plantations that produce some of the world's finest tea.

## Transport in Kericho

Kericho's tea industry drives significant business travel and local transport needs. The town's location along the Kericho-Nakuru highway makes it accessible.

### Taxi Services
- Town-wide taxi service for residents and visitors
- Tea plantation transport connections
- Inter-town routes to Nakuru, Kisumu, and Sotik
- Airport transfers

### Car Hire
- Self-drive vehicles for exploring the tea highlands
- Executive cars for tea industry executives
- Group transport for conferences and agricultural events
- Affordable weekly and monthly rates
