Karen is an upscale suburb of Nairobi known for its leafy residential areas, the Karen Blixen Museum, and the Nairobi National Park proximity.

## Transport in Karen

Karen residents and visitors demand premium transport services. The area's spread-out layout makes reliable taxi and car hire services essential for daily life.

### Taxi Services
- Executive transport for residents
- School runs and children's activities
- Shopping trips to Galleria Mall and The Hub
- Airport transfers

### Car Hire
- Luxury vehicles for Karen's upscale residents
- Self-drive SUVs for weekend getaways
- Chauffeur services for events and dinners
