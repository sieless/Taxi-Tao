Kitale is the agricultural hub of the North Rift region in Trans Nzoia County. It is known for its maize and wheat farming, as well as the Kitale Nature Conservancy.

## Transport in Kitale

Kitale serves as a key agricultural service center, with transport needs ranging from farm logistics to inter-county travel.

### Taxi Services
- Comprehensive town-wide taxi coverage
- Connections to Eldoret, Webuye, and other towns
- Airport transfers to Kitale Airstrip and Eldoret Airport
- 24/7 service for residents and businesses

### Car Hire
- Utility vehicles for agricultural transport
- Executive cars for government and business clients
- Self-drive options for exploring the North Rift
- Affordable long-term rental packages
