Eldoret Town is a major agricultural and academic center in the North Rift region of Kenya. It is home to Moi University and a thriving farming community.

## Transport in Eldoret

Eldoret's status as an educational and agricultural hub creates diverse transport needs for students, faculty, farmers, and business travelers.

### Taxi Services
- Campus transport for university students and staff
- Airport transfers to Eldoret International Airport
- Town-wide taxi service for residents
- Inter-town routes to Kitale, Iten, and Kapsabet

### Car Hire
- Economy cars for students and budget travelers
- Executive vehicles for business and government
- Utility vehicles for farm transport
- Long-term corporate rental packages
