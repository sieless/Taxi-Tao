Kisumu Town is the lakeside capital of Nyanza region and the third-largest city in Kenya. It sits on the shores of Lake Victoria.

## Transport in Kisumu

Kisumu is a growing commercial and tourism hub with the revitalization of the Lake Victoria waterfront and increasing business activity.

### Taxi Services
- City-wide transport within Kisumu
- Airport transfers to Kisumu International Airport
- Lake region connections to Homa Bay, Siaya, and Migori
- 24/7 availability

### Car Hire
- Self-drive vehicles for exploring the lake region
- Executive cars for business travelers
- Group transport for events and conferences
- Flexible daily, weekly, and monthly rates
